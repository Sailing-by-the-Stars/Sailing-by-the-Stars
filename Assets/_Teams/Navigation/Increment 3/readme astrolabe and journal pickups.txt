importing:
remove the astrolabe prefab under Player/Main Camera/
Drag the astrolabe and journal prefabs into the scene near the player.
undo all overrides on the sky prefab, and if it isn't already, move it to 0, 0, 0,

usage:
Walk to where the prefabs are placed and press E.
To hide the astrolabe, press tab
To "open" the journal press mouse1
To hide the journal, press j
